Starting a Business · Lesson 1

What Is a Business?

A business is more than selling something. At its foundation, a business identifies a need, creates value for people, and exchanges that value for money or another agreed form of value.

Learning path progress 1 of 6

Start with value

People and organizations have problems, needs, desires, goals, and tasks they want to accomplish. A business can create value by helping them solve those problems or achieve those goals.

A useful starting question is: “What problem can I help someone solve?”

The basic business cycle

  • Identify a real need or problem.
  • Understand the people who experience it.
  • Create a useful product, service, or solution.
  • Offer that solution clearly.
  • Receive payment when people choose to buy.
  • Improve the business based on experience and feedback.

A business does not have to begin as a large company. Someone can start with a small service, product, skill, creative work, local solution, or online offer and develop it gradually.

The goal is not simply to “make money.” A sustainable business needs to create enough value that customers have a reason to choose the offer and enough financial structure for the business to operate responsibly.
