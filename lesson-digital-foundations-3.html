Digital Safety

Before you build digital skills, it helps to understand the environment where those skills are used.

What is digital safety?

Digital safety means taking sensible steps to protect your accounts, personal information, devices and online activities.

As you use more digital services, you may create accounts, share information, communicate with other people and store important files online. Good safety habits help reduce avoidable risks.

The important idea

Digital safety is not about being afraid of technology. It is about using technology carefully and understanding how to protect yourself and your information.

Protect your accounts

Strong passwords Use strong, unique passwords for important accounts and avoid using the same password everywhere.
Account protection Use available security features such as two-factor authentication when appropriate.
Personal information Think carefully before sharing sensitive information such as passwords, financial details or private account information.
Suspicious messages Be cautious with unexpected links, attachments, requests for passwords or messages asking for urgent payments.

Think before you click

Not every message, website or link is trustworthy. Before clicking an unfamiliar link or providing information, check where it came from and whether the request makes sense.

Practical example

If you receive an unexpected message asking you to enter your password through a link, do not rush to respond. Verify the source and use the service's official website or application when possible.
