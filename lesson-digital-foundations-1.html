Understanding the Digital World

Before you build digital skills, it helps to understand the environment where those skills are used.

What is the digital world?

The digital world is the network of technologies, devices, software and online services that allow people to create, communicate, learn, work and exchange information.

Your phone, computer, internet connection, websites, applications and online accounts are all parts of this environment.

The important idea

Digital skills are not only about knowing how to use a device. They are about knowing how to use technology effectively to accomplish something.

Everyday examples

Communication Sending an email, joining a video call or communicating through a messaging platform.
Learning Taking an online course, researching information or practicing a new skill.
Creating Designing an image, writing a document, creating a video or building software.
Working Managing tasks, serving customers, collaborating with a team or delivering work online.
