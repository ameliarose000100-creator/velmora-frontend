Putting Digital Foundations Into Practice

Before you build digital skills, it helps to understand the environment where those skills are used.

Put what you have learned into action

Digital foundations become useful when you can apply them to real tasks. You do not need to know everything at once. The goal is to build good habits and use digital tools with purpose.

You have now explored digital communication, digital safety and the use of common digital tools. These foundations can support further learning and practical work.

The important idea

Learning becomes more valuable when you can use it. Start with a simple task, choose an appropriate tool, communicate clearly and review your work before sharing it.

A simple digital workflow

1. Understand the task Identify what you need to accomplish and what result you are expected to produce.
2. Choose the right tool Select a digital tool that fits the task instead of choosing a tool simply because it is popular.
3. Create and organize Complete the work carefully and keep your files, information and communication organized.
4. Review before sharing Check your work, confirm the information is appropriate and consider who will receive it before sending or sharing.

Your next step

Digital Foundations is only the beginning. As you continue through VELMORA, you can develop deeper skills, practice through real tasks and build evidence of what you can do.
