Using AI Effectively and Responsibly

AI becomes more useful when people know how to give it clear instructions, review its results and use it responsibly.

Give AI a Clear Task

When working with an AI tool, the quality of the instructions you provide can affect the usefulness of the result. A clear request tells the system what you want it to do.

Useful instructions can include the task, relevant context, the desired format and important limits or requirements.

A useful request

Instead of asking only for "a business plan," you could explain the type of business, the audience, the goal, the information you already have and the format you want.

Improve Results Through Iteration

Your first AI-generated result does not have to be the final result. You can review it, identify what needs improvement and give the system additional instructions.

This process of asking, reviewing and refining can help you move toward a result that better fits your actual needs.

Be specific Clearly explain the task and the result you are trying to achieve.
Add context Provide relevant information that helps the AI understand the situation.
Review Check whether the result is accurate, relevant and appropriate for your purpose.
Refine Give additional instructions when the first result does not meet your needs.
Check important information

AI systems can produce incorrect information, incomplete explanations or misleading results. Important facts should be checked against reliable sources before you rely on them.

Protect private information

Think carefully before entering personal, confidential or sensitive information into an AI service. Understand the service's privacy practices and avoid sharing information that you do not need to provide.

People remain responsible

Using AI does not remove human responsibility. The person using the tool remains responsible for reviewing important outputs and deciding how those outputs should be used.
