Practice · Mission 1

Practice professional communication.

Learning becomes useful when you can apply it. In this mission, practice responding clearly and professionally to a potential customer.

Communication

Reply to the customer

Imagine that you provide a service and a potential customer has contacted you for the first time.

Customer message
“Hello, I saw your service and I'm interested. How much does it cost and how long will it take?”
Think about being clear, respectful and helpful. Do not make promises you cannot support.
