AI in Everyday Life and Work

AI is already part of many digital services and workplaces. Understanding where it appears can help you recognize useful applications while also knowing when human judgment is needed.

AI Around You

You may interact with AI without always noticing it. Many digital products use AI or machine-learning systems to analyze information, recognize patterns or generate useful results.

Examples can include recommendation systems, search tools, spam filters, language tools, image features, navigation services and customer-support assistants.

AI can support many different tasks

AI is not limited to one type of job. Different systems can help people find information, organize work, recognize patterns, generate content or make predictions.

AI in Education

AI tools can help learners explain difficult ideas, practice skills, generate examples, summarize information and receive feedback.

However, learners still need to check important information and develop their own understanding. Using an AI tool is not the same as mastering a subject.

AI in Business and Work

Businesses can use AI to assist with tasks such as customer support, document analysis, forecasting, content creation, data organization and workflow automation.

The exact value of an AI system depends on the task, the quality of the information available and how people use and review its results.

Recommendations Systems can suggest products, videos, music or other content based on patterns in available information.
Assistants AI assistants can help answer questions, organize information or support certain tasks.
Creative tools AI can help generate or transform text, images, audio and other forms of creative content.
Business tools AI can assist with analysis, automation, customer service and other workplace activities.
Useful does not mean perfect

An AI tool can be useful while still having limitations. It may produce incorrect information, misunderstand a request or give an answer that requires additional context. People should review important outputs before relying on them.

Responsible use

Responsible AI use means understanding what a tool can do, checking important results, protecting private information and following the rules of the service or workplace where the tool is being used.
