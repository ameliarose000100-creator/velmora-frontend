How AI Generates and Predicts

AI can use patterns learned from data to produce results when it receives new information. Understanding this helps you use AI tools more carefully and effectively.

From Input to Output

After an AI system has been developed using data, it can receive new information and use patterns it has learned to produce an output.

Depending on the system and the task, that output might be a prediction, classification, recommendation, generated text, image, audio or another type of result.

The important idea

An AI system does not simply know the answer in the same way a person does. It uses patterns learned during development to produce a result for the information it receives.

A simple example

Imagine an AI system that has been developed using many examples of emails. It may learn patterns associated with different types of messages.

When a new email arrives, the system can use those learned patterns to predict or classify what type of message it might be.

Input The new information provided to an AI system, such as a question, image, email or other data.
Learned patterns Relationships or features identified from examples and data during the system's development.
Prediction A result produced by the system based on the patterns it has learned.
Generated output Content or another result produced by an AI system, such as text, an image or audio.
Prediction is not certainty

An AI-generated prediction or result is not automatically a fact. The system can produce an incorrect result, especially when the input is unusual, unclear, outside its learned patterns or affected by limitations in its development data.

Why human judgment matters

AI can be useful for generating ideas, finding patterns and helping with tasks, but people should review important results before relying on them. Human judgment remains important when accuracy, safety, money or other significant decisions are involved.
