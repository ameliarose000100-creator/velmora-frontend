Understanding Digital Communication

Before you build digital skills, it helps to understand the environment where those skills are used.

What is digital communication?

Digital communication is the exchange of information through digital devices, applications and online platforms.

It allows people to communicate across distance using tools such as email, messaging apps, video calls, websites and collaboration platforms.

The important idea

Good digital communication is more than sending a message. It means choosing the right tool, communicating clearly and considering the person who will receive the information.

Common forms of digital communication

Email Useful for formal communication, documents, applications and messages that may need to be referenced later.
Messaging Useful for quick conversations, updates, questions and everyday communication.
Video calls Useful when people need to speak directly, discuss ideas or work together remotely.
Collaboration platforms Useful for teams that need to share files, coordinate tasks and work on projects together.

Communicate clearly

Before sending a digital message, think about what you want the other person to understand. Use clear language, provide the important details and choose a communication method that fits the situation.

Practical example

If you are applying for a job, a professional email with the required information is generally more appropriate than sending a casual one-word message.
